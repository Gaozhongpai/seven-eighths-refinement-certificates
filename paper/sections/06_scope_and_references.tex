\section{The limit of the parameter refinement}

The perturbation is close to the limit of this particular balanced
witness estimate. At $y=0$ the zero-margin quadratic is
\[
 Q_p(\delta)=(2448+6048p)\delta^2+
              (-1896+11520p)\delta+370-22200p.
\]
Its discriminant test gives
\[
 4AC-B^2=-576(1162800p^2+286020p-49).
\]
The vertex remains inside $0\le\delta\le5/6$ near the present
perturbation. Therefore this estimate cannot support a larger
perturbation past
\[
 p_*=-\frac{1589}{12920}+\frac{7\sqrt{465053}}{38760}
       =0.0001711975385\ldots,
\]
whose loss-free crossing is approximately $0.8749572006$.
This is a limitation of the retained endpoint inequality, not a
lower bound for the true physical source or other arithmetic methods.
